Methodology

LCR: annualized land-consumption rate derived from built-up area change.

PGR: annualized population growth rate derived from census totals.

SDG 11.3.1: LCR divided by PGR where both are valid and PGR is non-zero. Verify units, geographic comparability, and the official indicator methodology before reporting.
