Methods & interpretation

Buffers: Turf.js geodesic circle approximations, in kilometres.

Proximity: nearest facility by straight-line distance from representative points.

Network route: OSRM driving route; returned distance and duration are estimates based on its routable road network, not observed travel times.

Access equity: point-in-buffer counts are indicative; population polygon totals should only be apportioned where appropriate population counts and polygon boundaries are available. No population is inferred from polygon area.
